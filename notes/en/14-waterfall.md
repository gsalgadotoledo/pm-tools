# 14 - Waterfall

## Agile vs Waterfall

| | Agile | Waterfall |
|---|---|---|
| **Approach** | Pick 2-5 features, build, release, validate, repeat | Build ALL features at once, release everything together |
| **Risk** | Low — validate early | High — no validation until the end |
| **Adaptability** | High — pivot anytime | Low — locked in until done |
| **Time to market** | Faster (incremental) | Slower (everything at once) |

## Why Waterfall is Risky

1. Takes a long time to develop everything → **competitors may move faster**
2. Market/user needs may **change** during development
3. You might build features users **don't actually want**
4. No feedback until it's all done → **expensive mistakes**
