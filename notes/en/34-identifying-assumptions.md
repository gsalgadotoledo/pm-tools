# 34 - Identifying Assumptions (MVP Step 2)

## Every Idea Is Built on Assumptions

Like starting a car — you assume the engine works, there's gas, it's not too cold. Every product idea has dozens of hidden assumptions.

## Two Ways to Find Your Assumptions

### Method 1: Lean Zen Mode
> "We don't know anything. Anything we think we know is an assumption."

Ask: What do I think I already know before I've even tested?

### Method 2: Fill in the Blank (more practical)
> "In order for this to be successful, the following must be true: ___"

Then list every factor that goes into potential success.

## The 4 Most Common Assumptions

| # | Assumption | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | My user has X problem | Bluetooth headphones: "people are fed up with cords" — but are they? |
| **2** | [X] matters to my customers | "People care about it being cheaper" — but maybe they care about quality more |
| **3** | Users will pay/sign up | Hot Pockets: "people will pay for a lava bean burrito" — is the problem big enough to pay for? |
| **4** | No satisfactory substitutes exist | Temperature app: people can just look out the window or check weather.com |

## Focus on the Big Ones

You could write out hundreds of assumptions. Focus on the ones that, if wrong, **sink the whole thing**.
