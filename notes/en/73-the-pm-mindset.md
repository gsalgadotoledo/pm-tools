# 73 - The PM Mindset (Course Conclusion)

## The Core Mindset

> You are almost always wrong — and so is everyone else. Until you prove it with data and users.

| Principle | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Prove through data** | Gut feelings are starting points, not decisions |
| **Best decisions with available info** | Don't wait for perfect data — decide with what you have |
| **Get more info if needed** | If data is insufficient, go get it (user interviews, metrics, experiments) |
| **Think deeper than gut feeling** | Challenge every assumption, including your own |

## It's a Mindset, Not Just Skills

Skills are necessary but not sufficient. The PM mindset is:
- Always asking **why**
- Always looking for **data** to support or disprove
- Always thinking about the **user's real problem**
- Always willing to be **proven wrong**
