# 72 - Your First Days as a PM

> New chapter: Excelling at the PM Role

PM has one of the **longest ramp-up times** of any job — you need to understand the history, goals, and challenges of every team you work with.

## 6 Tips for Your First Weeks

| # | Tip | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Schedule 1-on-1s with everyone** | Meet leaders of each major team. Ask: goals? challenges? Take notes |
| **2** | **Meet with the lead engineer** | Understand: architecture, databases, languages, biggest technical challenges, how data is stored |
| **3** | **Start talking to users** | Join sales calls, sit with customer service, search Twitter/Reddit for what people say about the company |
| **4** | **Read every internal document** | Past epic specs, mobile releases, sales decks, internal presentations → historical view of the product |
| **5** | **Look at the data** | Meet with data science/analysts. What metrics exist? What tracking is missing? |
| **6** | **Meet with the boss (Head of Product / CEO)** | Ask: expectations for your role, metrics to optimize, biggest fears for the next year, competitors, process improvements needed |

## Why Each Step Matters

| Step | What it gives you |
|---|---|
| 1-on-1s | Map of the org — who does what, where the friction is |
| Engineering deep dive | Know what's possible before proposing features |
| Talking to users | Your own conclusions, not inherited assumptions |
| Reading docs | History of decisions — avoid repeating mistakes |
| Data review | Know what you can measure and what's missing |
| Boss meeting | Alignment on expectations from day one |
