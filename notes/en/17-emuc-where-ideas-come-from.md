# 17 - EMUC: Where Ideas Come From

## The EMUC Framework

| Letter | Source | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| **E** | **Employees** | Ideas from coworkers, management, and yourself |
| **M** | **Metrics** | Problems/inefficiencies found in usage data (e.g., users spending 1 sec on a page then leaving) |
| **U** | **Users** | Feedback from forums, emails, social media — the people using your product |
| **C** | **Clients/Customers** | The people who **paid** for the product (not always the same as users — especially in B2B) |

## EMUC by PM Type

| PM Type | Primary source | Secondary sources |
|---|---|---|
| **Internal PM** | **Employees** (your coworkers are the stakeholders) | Metrics |
| **B2C PM** | **Users** | Metrics, Employees (especially management) |
| **B2B PM** | **Employees + Clients** | Users, Metrics |

## Key Insights

- Your job is **NOT** to be the "idea person"
- Ideas come from **everywhere** — you **collect and organize** them
- New ideas can **take precedence** over existing ones based on user feedback volume, competition, etc.
- **User ≠ Client** (especially in B2B — the person using it may not be the person who bought it)

## Buzzwords

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **EMUC** | Employees, Metrics, Users, Clients — the 4 sources of product ideas |
| **Top of funnel** | The starting point where all ideas enter before being filtered/prioritized |
| **Backlog** | The list of all ideas/features waiting to be evaluated and built |
