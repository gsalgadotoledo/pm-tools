# 04 - PM Roles: Details & Priorities

## Internal Product Manager

| Aspect | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Best for** | People starting out in product management |
| **Users** | Only people within your company (marketing, sales, etc.) |
| **Risk level** | Low — a buggy feature won't lose the company big money |
| **Learning focus** | Technology — integrating with internal systems and third-party tools |
| **Day-to-day** | Heavy on **project management** |
| **Platforms** | Usually one or very few |

**Third-party tools you'll work with**: marketing software, **CRMs**, internal systems.

---

## B2B / SaaS Product Manager

| Aspect | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Best for** | Also a good introduction to PM |
| **Users** | Hundreds to thousands of companies (not millions of individual users) |
| **Risk level** | Medium |
| **Flexibility** | More creative in **how** you build, less flexible on **what** you build |
| **Who sets priorities?** | Heavily weighted by the **sales team** and revenue potential |
| **Deadlines** | Tighter — tied to **sales deals** with deadlines |
| **Platforms** | Usually one or very few |

**Key dynamic**: Sales says "feature X will bring in $Y" — that drives your roadmap.

---

## B2C / Consumer Product Manager

| Aspect | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Best for** | People who don't mind **uncertainty** and **pressure** |
| **Users** | Millions or tens of millions |
| **Risk level** | High — bugs can cause massive user loss and revenue impact |
| **Flexibility** | High on what to build, but must validate through data |
| **Who sets priorities?** | No one tells you — you figure it out through research and testing |
| **Deadlines** | Tied to **release cycles** |
| **Platforms** | Multiple — Web, iOS, Android |
| **Learning** | Broad — from web development to mobile development |

**Key dynamic**: Extensive user testing, constant feedback analysis, **agile iteration**.

---

## Comparison at a Glance

| | Internal | B2B/SaaS | B2C |
|---|---|---|---|
| **User base size** | Tens/hundreds | Hundreds/thousands of companies | Millions |
| **Risk if things break** | Low | Medium | High |
| **Who drives priorities** | Internal needs | Sales team + revenue | User research + data |
| **Creative freedom** | Low | Medium (how, not what) | High (but must validate) |
| **Platforms** | 1 | 1-2 | Multiple (Web, iOS, Android) |
| **Good entry point?** | Yes | Yes | Challenging |
| **Pressure level** | Low | Medium | High |

---

## Buzzwords

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **CRM** | Customer Relationship Management — software to manage client relationships (e.g., Salesforce) |
| **Agile** | Iterative development methodology — build, test, learn, repeat in short cycles |
| **Release Cycle** | The scheduled timeframe for shipping new versions of the product |
| **Roadmap** | The plan of what features/products will be built and when |
| **Iterate** | Repeatedly refine a feature or product based on feedback and data |

---

## Tools & Techniques

| Tool/Technique | Context |
|---|---|
| **CRMs** (Salesforce, HubSpot) | Internal and B2B PMs integrate with these to manage client data |
| **Third-party integrations** | Internal PMs frequently connect internal tools with external services |
| **Agile methodology** | B2C PMs iterate and test rapidly to discover what to build |
| **User testing** | B2C PMs rely heavily on testing with real users before shipping |
| **Feedback loops** | B2C PMs continually review user feedback to inform priorities |
| **Project management** | Internal PMs spend significant time on project management workflows |
