# 21 - Finding Competitors

## Why Competitors Matter More Than Market Size

You can have the biggest market in the world — if it's full of strong competitors, your opportunity shrinks. And if a market is empty, you need to know **why**.

## The Two Sides of "No Competitors"

| Scenario | What it means |
|---|---|
| No one is doing this because **you're first** | Rare — but possible. Validate demand before celebrating |
| No one is doing this because **there's no demand** | Much more likely. Others already figured out it's a bad idea |

> Don't assume you're Steve Jobs. Assume the market knows something you don't — then prove otherwise.

## Feature Triage

As a PM, you decide which opportunity is most likely to:

1. **Get more users**
2. **Make existing users happier**
3. **Enhance the brand**

You **can't** make that call without knowing your competitors.

## Market Scenarios

| Market size | Competition | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| Big market | Many competitors | Opportunity exists but you must differentiate |
| Big market | Few competitors | High potential — but ask why few are there |
| Small market | Many competitors | Crowded niche — hard to win |
| Small market | Few competitors | Could be a good niche — or no demand |

## What You Need to Know Before Building

- Who are the competitors?
- What do they do that's similar to your proposal?
- Do any of them do it **better**?
- Know this **before** engaging your dev team and company resources

## Buzzwords

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Feature Triage** | Deciding which opportunity/feature/project to pursue based on potential impact |
| **Competitive Analysis** | Researching what competitors offer to inform your product decisions |
| **Differentiation** | What makes your product different/better than competitors |
