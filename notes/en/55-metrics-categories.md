# 55 - Metrics Categories

## The 5 Buckets

| Category | What it measures | Example metrics |
|---|---|---|
| **Growth & Activation** | How is the product growing? | New users/month, new users by source (SEO, app store, referral, social), activated users |
| **Retention** | Who's coming back? | Retained users, resurrected users, retention rate |
| **Engagement** | How are users interacting? | Tweets/day, likes, views (30sec+), newsfeed position clicks |
| **User Happiness** | How happy are users? | NPS score, customer complaints, app store rating |
| **Revenue** | How much money? | LTV, CAC, MRR, ARR |

## Key Definitions

### Activation
**Activated user** ≠ downloaded/visited. They signed up AND performed a key action (e.g., first tweet, first playlist).

### Retention Example
- 100 users activated last month → 60 returned this month → **60% retention rate**
- 40 left → you send push notification → all 40 come back → **100% resurrection rate**

### NPS (Net Promoter Score)
"How likely are you to recommend us? (1-10)" — anything under 7 counts as zero. High bar by design.

### Revenue Metrics

| Metric | Formula | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **LTV** (Lifetime Value) | Revenue per customer over a time period | $300/user/year |
| **CAC** (Customer Acquisition Cost) | Total marketing spend ÷ customers acquired | $50/customer |
| **MRR** (Monthly Recurring Revenue) | All subscribers × monthly price | 100 users × $10 = $1,000/mo |
| **ARR** (Annual Recurring Revenue) | MRR × 12 | $12,000/yr |

## The Customer Lifecycle Journey

```
Finding us → Signing up → Using us → Happy? → Paying? → Referring others?
  Growth    → Activation → Engagement → Happiness → Revenue → Viral Growth
```

## PM Team Size and Metrics

| Company size | Metrics responsibility |
|---|---|
| **Large** | You own 1 category or even 1 specific metric |
| **Small** | You own growth + retention + engagement + everything |
