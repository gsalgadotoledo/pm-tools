# 25 - Competitor Analysis: 5 Criteria (Part 2)

## All 5 Criteria

| # | Criteria | Key Question |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Product Core | How good is their engineering team? |
| 2 | User Base Size | How many users can they leverage? |
| **3** | **Design** | **How good are they at making beautiful, usable products?** |
| **4** | **Brand** | **How strong is their brand perception?** |
| **5** | **Speed** | **How fast can they ship?** |

---

## 3. Design

People use products that are **well-designed and easy to use** — even if alternatives exist.

| Their design vs yours | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Better** | Avoid spaces where design is the key differentiator |
| **Worse** | Your design quality becomes a competitive advantage |

**Examples:**
- **Apple** — so design-focused they threaten any market they enter (see: watch market)
- **Path** — competed with Facebook, reached 30M users largely because their product was more beautiful

## 4. Brand

Brand determines what you **can and can't do** in the market.

| Strong brand advantages | |
|---|---|
| Higher customer loyalty | People forgive mistakes from brands they love |
| Bring users to new markets | Launch anything and people follow |
| Charge higher prices | GoPro at $500 vs competitors at $300 — same product, stronger brand |
| More press and funding | Benefit of the doubt from media and investors |

| Brand as a limitation | |
|---|---|
| **Pigeonholed brands** can't branch out | Lexus can't make a TV. Sony can make anything |
| Weaker brands struggle to catch up | Stronger competitor gets more press, funding, and trust on every new initiative |

**Ask yourself:**
- What's the gap between your brand perception and theirs?
- What can your brand do that theirs can't?
- Are there weaknesses to exploit?

## 5. Speed

How fast can your competitor build and ship?

| Bigger company | Smaller company |
|---|---|
| More hierarchy, bureaucracy | More focused, more agile |
| Slower to execute | Faster to ship |
| Advantage: resources | Advantage: speed |

**Example**: Facebook (small, fast) ran circles around MySpace (big, slow) — MySpace couldn't keep up.

> If you're big and slow, avoid situations where success = launch date. A smaller, faster team will beat you.

---

## The 5 Criteria Summary

| Criteria | If competitor is stronger | If competitor is weaker |
|---|---|---|
| **Product Core** | Avoid engineering-heavy competition | Lean into engineering quality |
| **User Base** | They dominate on launch; find niches | You're more focused, can specialize |
| **Design** | Avoid design-differentiated spaces | Your UX becomes your moat |
| **Brand** | They get press, trust, premium pricing | Find what your brand enables that theirs can't |
| **Speed** | They ship faster; don't race them | Use your speed as an advantage |
