# 22 - How to Find Competitors

## Step 0: Define What You Solve

Before searching, answer two questions:

1. **What problem does this solve?** → e.g., "Hard to film action sequences with traditional cameras"
2. **For whom?** → e.g., "Thrill seekers and videographers" (NOT "my users" — be specific)

## Step 1: Capture

Set up a spreadsheet (Google Sheets, Excel) with two categories:

```
| A: Competitors          |
|-------------------------|
| KNOWN                   |
| 1. [competitor]         |
| 2. [competitor]         |
| 3. ...                  |
| UNKNOWN                 |
| 1. [competitor]         |
| 2. ...                  |
```

- **Known**: competitors you already know (if at a company, you should know these — if not, Google "[your company] versus")
- **Unknown**: the ones hiding in the bushes — no judgment, just collect

> Quick trick: Google "[YourCompany] versus" — autocomplete reveals your top competitors instantly.

## Step 2: Three Search Strategies

### Strategy 1: Channel the User's Complaint

Think: how would a frustrated user describe this problem?

| Example searches (GoPro) |
|---|
| "DSLR too big for action shots" |
| "keep breaking my camera skateboarding" |
| "camera too heavy for parachuting" |

**What you'll find:**
1. Companies using that same language in their marketing
2. People complaining online → conversations → how others solved it
3. Google's related connections

**Pro tip**: Use `site:reddit.com`, `site:quora.com` to search specific communities.

### Strategy 2: Literally Describe Your Product

Be a robot. Describe exactly what it does.

| Example searches (GoPro) |
|---|
| "tiny camera fits to your head shoots video" |
| "compact camera designed for action" |
| "camera you can attach to your head that films" |

**What you'll find:**
1. Companies using literal feature descriptions
2. **Ads at the top** — companies paying to be there = likely competitors
3. Google's related suggestions

**Pro tip**: Use `"quotation marks"` for exact match searches.

### Strategy 3: Pitch It in One Sentence

How would you pitch this product?

| Example searches (GoPro) |
|---|
| "world's smallest 4K camera" |
| "best action camera for extreme sports" |

Be **specific** — not "this app saves you time" but "save time by recording voice notes to Evernote."

---

## Summary

```
Define problem + who → Known competitors → 3 strategies for Unknown:
  1. User complaint language + site:reddit/quora
  2. Literal feature description + check ads
  3. One-sentence pitch
→ Add everything to your spreadsheet
```

## Buzzwords

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Market Intelligence** | Research on competitors and market conditions |
| **Known Competitors** | Competitors you and your company already know about |
| **Unknown Competitors** | Hidden competitors you discover through research |

## Tools

| Tool | Use |
|---|---|
| **Google Autocomplete** | "[company] versus" reveals known competitors |
| **Google `site:` operator** | Search within specific sites (reddit, quora) |
| **Google `"exact match"`** | Quotation marks for precise search results |
| **Reddit / Quora / Yahoo Answers** | Communities where users discuss problems and alternatives |
| **Google Ads (top results)** | Companies paying to appear = self-selected competitors |
