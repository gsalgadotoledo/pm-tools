# 10 - Product Development Process (Part 2)

## The 7 Phases (Complete)

```
Conceive -> Plan -> Develop -> Iterate -> Launch -> Steady State -> Maintain/Kill
                                         [-------- this note --------]
```

## Phase 5: Launch

- Work with **marketing**, **legal**, **PR**, and **sales** teams
- **Position** the product for public launch
- Release to the public and observe the reaction

## Phase 6: Steady State

- Collect **metrics** on usage and purchases
- **Analyze** and **optimize** metrics for maximum ROI
- Marketing continues to market, sales continues to sell
- Assess: should we keep moving forward with this?

## Phase 7: Maintain or Kill

Two questions to answer:

| Question | If YES | If NO |
|---|---|---|
| Is it paying for itself? (ROI) | Maintain | Consider killing |
| Does it fit our company vision? | Maintain | Kill even if profitable |
| Are we still competitive? | Maintain | Consider killing |

**Reasons to kill a product:**
- Poor revenue / not paying for itself
- No longer fits company vision or direction
- Market has changed, no longer competitive
- Maintenance cost too high relative to return

**When killing a product → Sunsetting:**
- Slow transition to end-of-life
- Message the user base about what's happening
- Create an **end-of-life plan** (e.g., let users export/backup their data)

## All 7 Phases Summary

| # | Phase | Key Activity |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Conceive** | Collect user problems, brainstorm solutions |
| 2 | **Plan** | Market research, business case, customer interviews, roadmap |
| 3 | **Develop** | Timelines, specs, user stories, estimations |
| 4 | **Iterate** | MVP, alpha/beta testing, validate assumptions |
| 5 | **Launch** | Coordinate with marketing/legal/PR/sales, go public |
| 6 | **Steady State** | Collect metrics, optimize, assess viability |
| 7 | **Maintain/Kill** | Keep investing or sunset the product |

---

## Buzzwords

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Sunsetting** | The process of slowly winding down and killing a product |
| **End-of-Life (EOL)** | The point where a product is no longer supported or available |
| **ROI** | Return on Investment — is the product earning more than it costs? |
| **Positioning** | How you present the product to the market relative to competitors |
