# 36 - Ranking Risky Assumptions (MVP Step 2b)

## Why Rank by Risk?

Test the riskiest assumptions **first**. Don't waste resources validating easy ones only to fail on assumption #6 and scrap everything.

## Risk Ranking of the 4 Common Assumptions

| Rank | Assumption | Risk Level | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1 (highest)** | My customer has X problem | **Critical** | If the problem doesn't exist, you're building a solution in search of a problem — game over |
| **2** | [X] matters to my customers | **High** | Setback, but marketing can convince people to care about things they didn't before (Domino's 30 min, HDTV resolution) |
| **3** | No satisfactory substitutes | **Medium** | People can be dislodged from substitutes if you show a better way (feature phones → iPhone) |
| **4 (lowest)** | Users will pay/sign up | **Lower** | Problem for business model, but alternatives exist (ads, freemium, indirect monetization) |

## The Rule

```
Start with the assumption that, if false, makes ALL other assumptions irrelevant.
    → That's your #1 target for the MVP experiment.
    → After validating it, move to the next riskiest.
```

## Key Insight

> A solution in search of a problem **never goes well**. Always validate the problem exists before anything else.
