# 65 - Communication Skills for PMs

> New chapter: Working with People & Stakeholders

## Why Communication is #1

Being a **communication hub** is the biggest part of the PM job. Everything that affects your product happens through communication.

## Communication Channels

| Channel | Tips |
|---|---|
| **Meetings** | Follow up with recap email + action items. Score major points by ensuring follow-through |
| **Conference/Video calls** | Clarity of verbal message. Share links to slides/stats in the meeting invite |
| **Emails** | Clear, concise, links to all data/resources referenced. This is your **primary method** |
| **Informal (lunch, coffee)** | Great for feedback, ideas, building support. Don't underestimate casual conversations |

## The Golden Rule

> "Six different audiences, six different slide decks."

Communicate **differently** with different types of people — engineers, designers, executives, sales, legal, users all need different messages.
