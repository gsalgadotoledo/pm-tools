# 47 - Email MVP: Deep Dive

## Best Suited For

Smaller organizations with **low brand anxiety**.

## Pros & Cons

| Pros | Cons |
|---|---|
| Very quick to execute | Can come off sloppy |
| Limit test group easily (statistically significant) | Not standard to ask people to buy via email |
| Segment users by usage stats/profile data | Risk of denting your brand if tone doesn't match |
| Cheap — no dev time needed | |
| Anyone can do it | |

## Tips for Success

| # | Tip |
|---|---|
| **1** | **Know your audience's tolerance** — luxury brand vs bargain outlet have different thresholds. AppSumo gets away with it because they sell flash deals to entrepreneurs used to beta products |
| **2** | **Match production value** — use the same templates, design, images, and tone as your normal email campaigns. Use tools like **Canva** for quick images |
| **3** | **Pair with another MVP technique** — email alone is tacky. Add a landing page link for details, or offer a concierge beta program in the email |

## Pairing Combinations

| Email + | How it works |
|---|---|
| **Landing Page** | Pitch in email → link to landing page for more info |
| **Concierge** | Ask them to reply with interest → manually run them through a beta |
| **Shadow Button** | Mention the feature → link to the product where the shadow button lives |
