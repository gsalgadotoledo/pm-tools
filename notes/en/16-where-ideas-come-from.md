# 16 - Where Do Ideas Come From?

> New chapter: Ideas, User Needs & Requirements

## The Myth

"PMs and executives sit in a room, come up with brilliant ideas, designers make it pretty, developers build it, executives get rich."

**This is completely wrong.**

## The Reality

Your job is **NOT** to be the "idea person." Your job is to:

1. **Receive** a huge influx of ideas and requirements from everywhere
2. **Evaluate** which are good and which are bad
3. **Decide** when is the best time to do them given company, tech, and market context

## Where Ideas & Requirements Come From

| Source | Type |
|---|---|
| **Sales team** | "We need this feature to close deals" |
| **Marketing team** | "We need this functionality" |
| **Users/customers** | Feedback, complaints, requests |
| **Executives** | Strategic direction |
| **Engineers** | Technical improvements |
| **Designers** | UX improvements |
| **Data/metrics** | Usage patterns reveal opportunities |
| **Market/competitors** | Industry trends |
| **You (PM)** | Your own observations and research |

## Key Insight

Users often think they need X when they actually need Y. Your job is to find the **real need** behind the request.

---

## Buzzwords

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Requirements** | Things other people/teams tell you they need built |
| **Ideas** | Potential features or solutions — need validation before building |
| **False needs** | What users say they want vs what they actually need |
