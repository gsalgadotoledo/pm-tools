# 57 - HEART Metrics Framework

## What is HEART?

A framework by Kerry Rodden (Google Ventures) for thinking through which metrics to track across the customer journey.

## The Acronym

| Letter | Category | Question |
|---|---|---|
| **H** | Happiness | How happy is your user? |
| **E** | Engagement | How engaged in the short term? |
| **A** | Adoption | How many users have tried the product? |
| **R** | Retention | Are users returning over time? |
| **T** | Task Success | Can users complete the key action? |

## Actual Customer Journey Order

```
Adoption → Task Success → Engagement → Retention → (Happiness measured anytime)
```

## The Table: Goals × Signals × Metrics

| | Goals | Signals | Metrics |
|---|---|---|---|
| **What** | What do you want to happen? | What do you need to measure? | How do you express it as a number? |

## Example: Uber for Spiked Lemonade

| Category | Goal | Signal | Metric |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Happiness** | Maximize drinker satisfaction with lemonade + delivery | Survey responses, ratings | NPS score, avg delivery rating |
| **Engagement** | Maximize order value and frequency in short periods | Orders placed, cart additions | Avg order value, orders/user/week |
| **Adoption** | Maximize people who ordered at least once | First-time orders | % of downloads that place first order |
| **Retention** | Maximize users returning monthly | Returning users | % of total users active month-over-month |
| **Task Success** | Minimize abandoned carts, maximize orders under 5 min | Cart completions, time to complete | Abandonment rate, % orders completed in <5 min |
