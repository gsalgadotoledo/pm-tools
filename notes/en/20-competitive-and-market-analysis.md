# 20 - Competitive & Market Analysis

> New chapter: Understanding your market before building.

## Two Approaches to Market Sizing

### Top Down (less reliable)

```
Total market → estimate your % share → projected revenue
```

- Example: 100K ice cream fans with iPhones × 10% penetration × $5 = $50K
- **Problem**: you're guessing your market penetration — always too optimistic

### Bottom Up (preferred)

```
Current sales of similar products → estimate what % you can capture
```

- Example: similar apps get 1,000 downloads/month × 5% capture = 50 downloads/month
- **Better** because it's based on real existing data, not assumptions

## Top Down vs Bottom Up

| | Top Down | Bottom Up |
|---|---|---|
| **Starts from** | Total market size | Existing sales of similar products |
| **Based on** | Assumptions | Real data and trends |
| **Accuracy** | Optimistic | Conservative and realistic |
| **Effort** | Quick | Takes more research |
| **Respected?** | Less | More |

> Always better to be **conservative** than overly optimistic.

## Tools for Market Research

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Google Search** | Search "industry report for X" or "market size for X" to find existing reports |
| **Compete.com** | Compare traffic volumes across competitor websites |
| **Google AdWords Keyword Tool** | See search volumes and related terms — shows real interest/demand |
| **Twitter / Reddit** | See what people say about products in your space — frustrations = opportunities |
| **App Annie** | App store sales volumes and popularity data |

## Buzzwords

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Market Sizing** | Estimating how big the opportunity is |
| **Top Down** | Start from total market, estimate your share |
| **Bottom Up** | Start from existing sales, estimate what you can capture |
| **Market Penetration** | % of the total market you can realistically reach |
| **TAM** | Total Addressable Market (the "top" in top-down) |
