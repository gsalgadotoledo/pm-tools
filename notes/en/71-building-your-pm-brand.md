# 71 - Building Your Personal PM Brand

## Why It Matters

Recruiters check your online presence before interviews. An online record of PM knowledge = credibility before you walk through the door.

## What to Build

| Asset | Why |
|---|---|
| **Personal website/blog** | Central hub for your PM thinking |
| **Twitter/X account** | Participate in product discussions publicly |
| **Quora answers** | Answer questions on products and strategy — builds authority |
| **Side project portfolio** | Show, don't just tell |

## Blog Post Ideas

| # | Topic |
|---|---|
| 1 | Why a product you use is poor + how to improve it (common PM interview question) |
| 2 | Top 5 mistakes in your current role + what you learned |
| 3 | Book reviews of PM books — what you agree/disagree with |
| 4 | Thoughts on recent tech trends — how they'll change the market |
| 5 | Redesign of a feature — problem, research, proposed solution |

## The Advantage

Since there's **not as much content** about PM compared to engineering or design, it's actually **pretty easy to stand out** online.
