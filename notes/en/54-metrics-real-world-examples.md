# 54 - Metrics: Real World Examples

## Twitter

| Category | Metrics |
|---|---|
| **Growth** | Total new users/month, MAU, DAU, activated users |
| **Engagement** | Multiple logins/day, time spent, tweets sent/user, avg likes, avg retweets, avg follows, DMs sent |

## YouTube

| Category | Metrics |
|---|---|
| **Growth** | New users, MAU, DAU, activated users |
| **Engagement** | Video views/user (must watch 30+ sec), avg viewing time/user (1 long video > 20 short ones) |

## Facebook

| Category | Metrics |
|---|---|
| **Growth** | New users, MAU, DAU, activated users |
| **Engagement** | Newsfeed position clicks (lower = bad relevancy), messages sent, time on site, avg likes given/day, avg likes received/post |

**Facebook trick**: They hold posts with zero likes in the newsfeed longer so users get a minimum threshold of engagement — otherwise users stop posting.

## Key Insight

- **Growth metrics** are similar across companies (new users, MAU, DAU)
- **Engagement metrics** are unique to each company/industry
- Even the same metric (video views) is defined differently: YouTube = 30 sec, Facebook = 3 sec
