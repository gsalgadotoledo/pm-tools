# 11 - Lean Philosophy

## What is Lean?

A product development philosophy: **cut out all unnecessary work until you're absolutely sure you need it.**

## The Food Delivery Example

| Approach | Traditional | Lean |
|---|---|---|
| Phone number | Buy a business number | Use your own cell phone |
| Advertising | Pay for ads | Put out flyers yourself |
| Delivery | Hire drivers | Drive the food yourself |
| When to scale | Before validating demand | Only when you can't handle it alone |

**The Lean rule**: do it yourself first. Only spend money/hire when demand proves you must.

## Core Idea

```
Don't spend resources until you KNOW you have to.
           |
           v
  Validate demand first → Then scale
```

## Lean vs Agile

| | Lean | Agile |
|---|---|---|
| **Scope** | Any business/product | Specifically for software |
| **Focus** | Eliminate waste, validate before investing | Iterative development in short cycles |
| **Relationship** | Lean is the philosophy | Agile is the software implementation |
