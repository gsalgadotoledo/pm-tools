# 07 - Product Lifecycle

## The 4 Phases

```
Revenue
  ^
  |          ___________
  |         /  Maturity  \
  |        /              \
  |       / Growth    Decline\
  |      /                    \
  |_____/ Introduction         \____
  +-----------------------------------> Time
```

| Phase | Revenue | Competition | Users | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Introduction** | Losing money | Little to none | Early adopters only | Product enters the market for the first time |
| **Growth** | Rising | Few challengers | Expanding from early adopters | Product accepted by market, improvements to stay competitive |
| **Maturity** | Peak/pinnacle | Many competitors | Mass market | Sales peak, competition gets intense, hard to differentiate |
| **Decline** | Diminishing | Saturated | Shrinking | Market sees product as old/irrelevant, phased out |

## How You Know Which Phase You're In

- Revenue over time
- How fast revenue is growing
- **MRR** (Monthly Recurring Revenue)

## Key Insight

There's **no definitive guide** to tell exactly where a company/product is within these phases. It's a **thinking framework**, not a precise measurement.

---

## Buzzwords

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Product Lifecycle** | The 4 stages a product goes through: Introduction, Growth, Maturity, Decline |
| **Early Adopters** | First users who try a product before the mainstream market |
| **MRR** | Monthly Recurring Revenue — predictable revenue earned each month |
| **Saturation Point** | When the market can no longer absorb more of the product |
| **Market Fit** | When a product satisfies a strong market demand (implicit in growth phase) |

## Frameworks

| Framework | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Product Lifecycle Model** | 4-phase framework (Introduction -> Growth -> Maturity -> Decline) to understand where a product/company stands in the market over time |
