# Tools & Platforms: 2026 Update

> Reference page — linked from MVP and wireframing notes.

## What Died or Changed

| Tool | Status | What happened |
|---|---|---|
| **InVision** | **DEAD (Dec 2024)** | Once valued at $2B, shut down completely. Freehand acquired by Miro. All user data deleted. |
| **Adobe XD** | **Discontinued** | No longer available for new purchase since June 2023. Maintenance mode only. |
| **POP (Prototyping on Paper)** | **Effectively dead** | Acquired by Marvel in 2016. Last update Oct 2023. No active development. |
| **Keynotopia** | **Irrelevant** | Keynote prototyping workflow fully superseded by Figma. |
| **HotGloo** | **Zombie** | Website up, but no meaningful activity for over a year. |

## What Survived (Niche)

| Tool | Status | Why it survives |
|---|---|---|
| **Sketch** | Active, declining | Mac-only. Lost massive ground to Figma but still has loyal users. |
| **Axure** | Active, specialized | Go-to for complex enterprise prototypes (conditional logic, variables). Figma can't match its depth. |
| **Balsamiq** | Active, focused | Still the best dedicated low-fi wireframing tool. Cloud UI overhauled for 2026. |
| **UXPin** | Active, differentiated | Code-based prototypes (real HTML/CSS/JS). Used by Sony, PayPal, Netflix. |
| **Proto.io** | Active | High-fidelity interactive prototyping, focused on mobile. |

## The New Standard: Figma

| Stat | Number |
|---|---|
| Market share | **40.65%** (2026) |
| Monthly active users | **13 million** |
| Fortune 500 adoption | **~95%** |
| Revenue (2024) | **$749M** (48% growth) |
| IPO | July 2025, market cap ~$45B |

Figma now handles wireframing, mockups, AND prototyping. It replaced InVision, Adobe XD, and most Sketch workflows.

## Canva: Now a Creative Operating System

Launched "Visual Suite 2.0" (2025): presentations, videos, whiteboards, websites, forms, email design. Released their own AI design model. Acquired Affinity (vector/photo/layout, now free). Far beyond "quick design tool."

## Analytics & Metrics Tools (2026 Update)

### What Changed from the Original List

| Original Tool | Status | Replacement |
|---|---|---|
| **Google Analytics** | **GA4 is the standard** — Universal Analytics fully sunset July 2023 | Still GA, but it's GA4 now |
| **Crazy Egg** | Active but niche | **Hotjar** and **Microsoft Clarity** (free) are more popular |
| **KISSmetrics** | **Largely faded** — brand exists but most users migrated | **Mixpanel**, **Amplitude**, or **PostHog** |
| **Mixpanel** | **Still strong** — generous free tier, competing with Amplitude | Still recommended |
| **Optimizely** | **Enterprise-only** — acquired by Episerver, expensive | **PostHog**, **Statsig**, **GrowthBook** for smaller teams |
| **Segment** | **Still the leader** — acquired by Twilio for $3.2B | **RudderStack** (open-source alternative) gaining traction |

### AI-Powered Analytics (New)
| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Amplitude AI** | Natural language querying, AI-generated insights |
| **Mixpanel Spark** | AI assistant — ask data questions in plain English |
| **PostHog AI** | AI insight generation |
| **GA4 AI Insights** | Automated anomaly detection |
| **Statsig Autotune** | AI auto-optimization of experiments |

### Key Trends
1. **Contentsquare consolidation** — acquired Hotjar (2021) + Heap (2023)
2. **PostHog as disruptor** — open-source all-in-one pulling budget from multiple point solutions
3. **Privacy-first analytics rising** — Plausible, Fathom, Matomo gaining share as regulations tighten
4. **Warehouse-native tools** — Hightouch, Eppo building on your data warehouse instead of duplicating data
5. **AI = table stakes** — every tool now has natural language querying; differentiation is in **predictive insights**
6. **Microsoft Clarity is free** — no excuse not to have heatmaps/session replay

## Key Takeaways for PMs in 2026

1. **Figma is the standard** — if you're not using it, you're in the minority
2. **InVision and Adobe XD are gone** — don't invest in either
3. **AI-first prototyping is mainstream** — non-technical PMs can generate working apps from prompts
4. **No-code MVP tools are production-ready** — Bubble, Webflow, Glide replace the Frankenstein approach
5. **The "vibe coding" era is here** — 58% of PMs use no-code or AI prototyping (2026 Tenet report)
