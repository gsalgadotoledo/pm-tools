# 30 - Tracking Competitors Over Time

## 3 Events to Watch For

| Event | Why it matters | Effect on 5 Criteria |
|---|---|---|
| **Funding** | More money = more hires, more ads, faster execution | Speed, Product Core, User Base |
| **Acquisitions** | They absorb teams, users, or design talent | Product Core, User Base, Design |
| **Feature/Product Launches** | Direct competitive moves — especially if they copy you | All 5 criteria |

## 3 Tools to Track Competitors

| Tool | What it does | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **Crunchbase** | Tracks funding rounds and acquisitions | Funding, Acquisitions — follow companies for email alerts |
| **Mention.com** | Monitors social media, blogs, news for any competitor activity | Feature launches, brand perception, seismic shifts |
| **Google Alerts** | Emails you when new search results appear for a keyword | Broadest sweep — SEO changes, conversations, news |

## The 5 Criteria Are Always Changing

Your competitors are constantly making moves that shift their position. Stay updated on:
1. Product Core — did they hire/acquire a great team?
2. User Base — are they growing fast?
3. Design — did they improve their UX?
4. Brand — is their perception changing?
5. Speed — are they shipping faster or slower?
