# 48 - MVPs at Big Companies & The One Feature MVP

## The MVP Spectrum: Brand vs Resources

```
← Most resource-friendly          Most brand-friendly →
   Email MVP → Shadow Button → Landing Page → Concierge → Piecemeal → Wizard of Oz → One Feature
   (reckless with brand)                                              (protective of brand)
```

## Startup vs Big Company Risk

| | Startup | Big Company |
|---|---|---|
| **Main risk** | Running out of resources | Damaging brand |
| **Can tolerate** | Brand damage (who cares if you're dead?) | Wasted resources (they have plenty) |
| **MVP style** | Scrappy — email, landing page, concierge | Polished — One Feature MVP or Wizard of Oz |

## When You're "Beyond the Tactics"

At a certain size, companies can't use parlor tricks. You must **actually build something** — just the absolute minimum.

## The One Feature MVP (Type 9)

Build **literally one core function** and test its reception before adding more.

### How to Do It

1. List every function/feature in your product
2. Rank by **value to user**
3. One will stand out way more than the rest → **build only that**

### Example: Foursquare

| Full vision | MVP (one feature) |
|---|---|
| Check-ins, prizes, networking, badges, photos, hashtags, location-based everything | **Just check-ins** — connect social networks, check in, sends text to friends |

Everything else (photos, badges, hashtags) was secondary to the check-in process.

## The PM's Job with MVPs

1. **Gauge company tolerance**: do they care more about resources or brand?
2. **Pick the right MVP type** on the spectrum
3. **Optimize**: minimum resource expenditure for maximum validated learning

## Complete MVP Types (Final)

| # | Type | Effort | Brand Risk | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Email | Lowest | Highest | Small orgs, quick validation |
| 2 | Shadow Button | Low | High | PMs triaging features |
| 3 | 404 / Coming Soon | Low-Med | High | Testing demand |
| 4 | Explainer Video | Medium | Medium | Pre-product (Dropbox) |
| 5 | Landing Page | Medium | Medium | Gold standard for entrepreneurs |
| 6 | Concierge | Medium | Low-Med | Service-based products |
| 7 | Piecemeal | Medium | Low-Med | Frankenstein validation |
| 8 | Wizard of Oz | Med-High | Low | Real-looking front-end needed |
| 9 | **One Feature** | **Highest** | **Lowest** | **Big companies with brand to protect** |
