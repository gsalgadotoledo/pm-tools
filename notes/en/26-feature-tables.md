# 26 - Feature Tables

## What is a Feature Table?

A comparison chart: **your product vs competitors** across multiple dimensions.

```
|                    | You | Competitor A | Competitor B | Competitor C |
|--------------------|-----|-------------|-------------|-------------|
| Feature 1          | ✓   | ✓           | ✗           | ✓           |
| Feature 2          | ✓   | ✗           | ✓           | ✗           |
| Feature 3          | ✗   | ✓           | ✓           | ✓           |
| Price              | $99 | $149        | $79         | $129        |
| Platform           | All | Web only    | iOS only    | All         |
```

- **X axis (columns)**: Your product + competitors
- **Y axis (rows)**: Dimensions/features to compare

## Why Make Feature Tables?

| Reason | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Know what's cutting edge** | Before launching, know what already exists |
| **Quick visual** | Everyone on your team can instantly see: are we competitive? |
| **Can't track mentally** | Too many competitors, too many features (GoPro has 50+ competitors) |
| **Avoid launching worse** | If your product is worse than what's out there, you'll probably fail |

## When to Use

- Before coming up with product ideas
- Before launching products
- As a living document for your team to reference

> You don't *have* to make these on the job, but knowing how is an **immensely valuable asset**.

---

## Buzzwords

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Feature Table** | Comparison chart of your product vs competitors across multiple dimensions |
| **Feature Parity** | When your product matches competitors on a specific feature |
| **Competitive Gap** | Features competitors have that you don't |
