# 28 - Feature Table: GoPro Example

## The Table

| Factor | GoPro Hero4 | TomTom Bandit | Veho | Sony FDR |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Price** | $499 | $349 | $200 | $399 |
| **Aesthetics** | 7/10 | 4/10 | 6/10 | 6/10 |
| **Weight** | 89g | 190g | 84g | 114g |
| **4K** | Yes (30fps) | Yes (12fps) | No | Yes (30fps) |
| **Max FPS** | 120 (1080p) | 60 | 60 | 120 |
| **Accessories** | 100+ | ~10 | ~15 | ~25 |

## How Factors Were Chosen

Target customer: **thrill seekers who film action** (broke surfers, extreme sports)

| Factor | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Price | Customers don't want to overspend |
| Aesthetics | Cool factor — vain extreme sports people |
| Weight | Worn on head/chest — can't be heavy |
| 4K | Higher resolution = cooler footage |
| Max FPS | Slow motion for action shots |
| Accessories | Need mounting options (head, chest, wrist) |

## What Was Deliberately Ignored

| Ignored factor | Why |
|---|---|
| Battery life | Users film in short bursts, not long sessions |
| On-camera editing | Users will edit montages on a computer |
| Camera controls/scrubbing | Just press record and go |

## Questions to Answer From the Table

1. Are we beating our direct competitors?
2. Are we the obvious choice?
3. Are we only appealing to one segment?
4. Where should we focus on improving?
5. What should we entirely ignore?

## How to Get This Data

- **Competitor websites** — they list all specs
- **Top 10 comparison websites** — quick overview
- **Consumer reviews** — real user experience
- **Actually use their product** — no substitute for hands-on
