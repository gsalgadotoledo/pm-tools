# 53 - Introduction to Metrics

> New chapter: Metrics

## What Are Metrics?

A fancy term for a **number that describes what's going on with your product**. Also called KPIs (Key Performance Indicators).

> "What gets measured gets managed." — Peter Drucker

## Why Metrics Matter

**Feedback loops**: The more frequently you get accurate feedback, the more effectively you manage.

| Example | Feedback | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Radar speed signs | Instant speed feedback | Reduces speeding more than tickets |
| Fitness trackers | Hourly calorie/activity data | Helps people lose weight |
| Product metrics | Daily/weekly product data | Helps PMs build better products |

## Defining Success

Before every endeavor, you must define: **what number means we succeeded?**

## Example: Facebook Comments

```
Company goal: Increase time spent on Facebook
    ↓
Data insight: People who comment more stay longer
    ↓
PM goal: Increase comments per person by 5%
    ↓
Build features that encourage commenting
    ↓
Measure: Did comments increase? Did time spent increase?
```
