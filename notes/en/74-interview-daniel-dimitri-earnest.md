# 74 - Interview: Daniel Dimitri (Head of Product, Earnest)

## His Background
- CS + Economics at Harvard → Private equity (1.5 yrs) → Google APM Program → Earnest (first PM, grew team to 8)

## Key Insights

### What is Product Management?
> "Filling the gaps between cross-functional teams." Creating shared timelines, shared understanding of what's being delivered, when, and why.

### The Google APM Program
- 30 people/year globally, 0-3 years out of college (usually 0)
- Requires CS background
- Rotations across teams (ads, maps, search, etc.)
- ~50% retention within 5 years
- You can request teams but they may ignore you

### B2B vs B2C PM is Very Different
- **DoubleClick (B2B)**: Features prioritized by what customers ask for, sales-driven
- **Google Maps (B2C)**: Log analysis, user behavior data, bug closing, feature requests

### Real Example: Dig Deeper Than Feature Requests
**Earnest operations team**: Asked for "pull documents faster" (reviewing tax PDFs).
**Real need**: Extract the key info from documents and automate the calculations — not make PDF reviewing faster.

> Classic Steve Jobs / Henry Ford: "If you asked people what they want, they'd say a faster horse."

### Real Example: Removing Features Can Backfire
**Google Maps**: Removed "Search Nearby" feature thinking users could just type "bars near X". It became their **#1 customer complaint**. Had to add it back.

> Google has a whole team in India that clusters user feedback for PMs to review — the only way to digest the volume.

### Google's Approach: Supply-Side Driven
Instead of "what do users want?" → "What can we do with all this technology?"
- Knowledge Graph → integrated into Maps, Search, Android
- This is different from user-research-driven companies

### 4 Skills He Looks For (Hiring)

| Skill | What he wants to see |
|---|---|
| **Analytical** | SQL proficiency (advanced preferred), data-driven thinking, statistics basics |
| **Technical** | CS fundamentals, understanding of build process, git, CI, what "build failed" means |
| **Creative** | Problem-solving in whiteboard interviews, creative thinking |
| **Collaborative** | Group projects, leadership roles, cross-functional experience |

### Communication = Accuracy + Precision + Brevity
| Quality | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Accuracy** | What you're saying is TRUE (not inferences you're uncertain about) |
| **Precision** | Unambiguous — can't be misinterpreted |
| **Brevity** | Don't bury truth in noise — be concise |

+ **Translation**: Use different words for different teams (legal vs engineering vs marketing)

### Entry-Level vs Senior Hiring
| Level | What they look for |
|---|---|
| **Entry** | Basic proficiency in the 4 skills, curiosity, academic signals |
| **Mid/Senior** | Actual PM experience with the types of stakeholders they'll engage |

### Portfolio vs Experience
> Independent portfolio doesn't demonstrate you can communicate across teams. They want to see **experience working with designers, engineers, legal, marketing** — not solo work.
