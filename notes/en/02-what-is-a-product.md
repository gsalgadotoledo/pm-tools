# 02 - What is a Product?

## Key Takeaways

### 1. A product can be anything

- A keyboard, a laptop, a shirt, a web browser — all products.
- A PM manages a given product.

### 2. PMs don't always own the entire product

- In startups and large companies, PMs own **sections/features** of what consumers see as one product.
- In PM lingo, each feature **is** a product.

### 3. Example: Facebook

Each of these is a separate "product" with its own PM and product team:

- Photos
- Newsfeed
- User Profile
- Messaging
- Comments

### 4. Products can be split even further

- Facebook's **Newsfeed** alone has multiple PMs:
  - One for the **ranking algorithm**
  - One for **advertisements**
  - Others for different newsfeed components

### 5. PMs can also be split by platform

Instead of by feature, some companies split PMs by:

- **Android** app
- **iOS** app
- **Web**

### 6. PMs can also own business model areas

- Advertising
- Subscription services
- Other revenue-related products

---

## Summary

A "product" for a PM can range from an **entire consumer-facing product** to a **single feature or component** within a larger system. The scope depends on the company size and complexity.
