## Key Terms by Category

### Metrics & KPIs
| Term | What it means |
|---|---|
| **KPI** | Key Performance Indicator — the number that tells you if you're winning or losing |
| **North Star Metric** | The ONE metric that best captures the core value your product delivers |
| **Conversion Rate** | % of users who complete a desired action (signup, checkout, etc.) |
| **Retention** | % of users who come back after first use |
| **Churn** | % of users who stop using the product |
| **DAU/MAU** | Daily/Monthly Active Users |
| **A/B Testing** | Compare two versions to see which performs better on a metric |

### Strategy & Planning
| Term | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Roadmap** | The plan of what gets built and when |
| **Mission** | The company/product's reason for existing — the "why" |
| **Vision** | Where the product is headed long-term |
| **Quarter (Q1-Q4)** | 3-month planning cycle — most companies set goals per quarter |
| **OKR** | Objectives and Key Results — goal-setting framework |
| **Budget** | Money allocated to a project or initiative |
| **Timeline** | The schedule for delivering a project or feature |
| **Estimation** | Forecasting effort, time, or cost for a feature or project |

### Discovery & Research
| Term | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Pain Points** | Problems or frustrations users experience with a product |
| **User Research** | Talking to and observing users to understand their needs |
| **User Testing** | Having real users try your product to find issues |
| **Rationale** | The reasoning behind a decision — the "why" you chose this approach |
| **Stakeholders** | People who have input or interest in what you're building |

### Organization & Prioritization
| Term | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Buckets** | Categories to group features, tasks, or ideas (e.g., "growth", "tech debt", "bugs") |
| **Backlog** | The list of all features/tasks waiting to be worked on |
| **Sprint** | A short (1-2 week) work cycle in Agile methodology |
| **MVP** | Minimum Viable Product — the simplest version that delivers value |
| **Scope** | The boundaries of what's included in a project or feature |

### Buzzwords You'll Hear Constantly
| Term | What it means |
|---|---|
| **SaaS** | Software as a Service |
| **B2B / B2C** | Business-to-Business / Business-to-Consumer |
| **Agile** | Iterative development — build, test, learn, repeat |
| **Iterate** | Refine repeatedly based on feedback |
| **Ship** | Release/deploy a feature to users |
| **Leverage** | Use something to your advantage (often overused) |
| **Align** | Get everyone on the same page |
| **Circle back** | Revisit a topic later |
| **Low-hanging fruit** | Easy wins with minimal effort |
| **Move the needle** | Make a meaningful impact on a metric |

### Frameworks & Mental Models
| Framework | What it is | When to use it |
|---|---|---|
| **Product Lifecycle** | 4 phases: Introduction -> Growth -> Maturity -> Decline | To understand where a company/product stands in the market and adapt your PM strategy accordingly — what you prioritize in Introduction (finding market fit) is completely different from Maturity (defending against competitors) |

### Things to Keep in Mind
- You are NOT anyone's boss — influence without authority
- If something is wrong with the product, it's YOUR fault
- Engineers and designers are your partners, not your employees
- "What do you actually do?" — you'll hear this a lot, get comfortable with it
- Project management is a skill you need, but it's not your job title

### Internal Jokes in Product Management
- *"That's the person that stops sales and legal from emailing you"* — how engineers describe PMs
- The PM Venn Diagram (UX + Tech + Business) with "You are here" in the center — your entire existence
- *"Can you just add one more thing?"* — scope creep in one sentence
- *"Let's take this offline"* — the meeting about the meeting
- *"Per my last email..."* — PM passive-aggressive mode activated
- *"We need to be more data-driven"* — said before ignoring the data
- *"What's the MVP?"* — asked about everything, even lunch plans
